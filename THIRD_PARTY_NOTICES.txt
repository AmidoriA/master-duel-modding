Floowandereeze and Modding (inspiration — please credit)
--------------------------------------------------------
- Qt app: https://github.com/Nauder/floowandereeze-and-modding-qt
- Catalog ETL: https://github.com/Nauder/floowandereeze-and-modding-etl
- License: GPL-3.0
- Floowandereeze and Modding (by Nauder) pioneered practical Master Duel card-art
  replacement: path layout (LocalData/<id>/0000/...), Texture2D -> RGBA32, LZ4
  pack, and backups. AmidoriA's C# tool reimplements related behaviors
  independently with AssetsTools.NET and does not copy Floowandereeze Python
  source. Please give Floowandereeze the credit it deserves.
- The shipped database.db is not a verbatim copy of upstream ETL output.
  Schema and contents are maintained in this project (Tools -> DB update from a
  local Master Duel install), including columns such as card_type and
  created_at. Early catalog ideas were inspired by Floowandereeze; treat the DB
  as this project's own artifact going forward.
- If you distribute a combined work that incorporates Floowandereeze GPL code,
  comply with GPL-3.0 for that combined work.
